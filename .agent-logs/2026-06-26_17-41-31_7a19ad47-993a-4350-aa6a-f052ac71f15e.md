Since Supabase enforces email confirmation by default, you have two options to proceed:

3. Toggle off **Confirm email** and click **Save**.

#### Option B: Manually Confirm a User (via SQL)
If you already created a user and want to confirm them immediately, open the **SQL Editor** in your Supabase dashboard and run:
```sql
UPDATE auth.users 
SET email_confirmed_at = NOW(), confirmed_at = NOW() 
WHERE email = 'your-email@example.com';
```
