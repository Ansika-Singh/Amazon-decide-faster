# 🌐 UnifyTalk — Pitch-Ready Product & Technical Dossier
> **"Talk to Anyone. Understood by All."**  
> *A unified, multi-modal, AI-powered communication ecosystem bridging the gap between Deaf, Mute, Blind, and Neurodivergent individuals and the hearing/speaking world.*

---

## 1. 🎯 The Executive Pitch

### The Problem
Over **1.5 billion people** globally live with some form of sensory, speech, or visual disability:
- **Deaf & Hard of Hearing** face isolation when communicating with non-signers.
- **Mute & Speech-Impaired** individuals struggle in daily transactions and emergencies where typing or sign language isn't recognized.
- **Blind & Low-Vision** people encounter inaccessible digital interfaces that fail WCAG standards and lack Braille/voice feedback.
- **Cross-Disability Disconnect:** Existing tools only solve for *one* disability in silos (e.g., just a transcription app or just a screen reader). A deaf person cannot easily talk with a blind person.

### The Solution: UnifyTalk
**UnifyTalk** is a real-time, unified accessibility web platform. It eliminates communication barriers by combining **on-device Computer Vision (MediaPipe)**, **Real-Time WebSockets**, **Web Speech Synthesis/Recognition**, **AAC Eye-Gaze Tracking**, **Grade 1 Braille translation**, and **multilingual auto-translation** into a single cohesive interface.

## 3. 🚀 Comprehensive Feature Breakdown (Phased Architecture)

### 💬 Phase 1: Adaptive Multi-Modal Chat (`/chat`)
- **Three Core Disability Modes:**
  - **Deaf Mode:** Converts incoming spoken audio into real-time visual subtitles; speech-to-text input with live captions.
  - **Mute Mode:** Converts typed text into natural speech synthesis (TTS) at the press of a key or send button.
  - **Blind Mode:** High-contrast audio-first layout with automatic voice feedback, screen-reader optimized elements, and spoken transcripts.
- **WebSocket Real-Time Rooms:** Instant peer-to-peer or group communication, live typing indicators, and message read receipts.
- **Chat Session Archival:** Saves and aggregates conversation history with full deletion and replay capabilities.

### 🖼️ Phase 2: AAC Pictogram Board with Eye-Gaze Tracking (`/board`)
- **Augmentative & Alternative Communication (AAC):** A collection of 76+ curated pictograms across 10 vital categories (Greetings, Needs, Feelings, Actions, Places, People, Time, Food, Medical).
- **Interactive Sentence Builder:** Users click or gaze-select symbols to build structured sentences with instant vocalization (`WebSpeech TTS`) and clipboard export.
- **Hands-Free Eye-Gaze Dwell Selection:**
  - Employs **MediaPipe FaceMesh** iris tracking (landmarks 468 & 473) via the user's webcam.
  - Computes gaze intersection and provides a visual dwell progress indicator (dwell timer: 1000ms) to trigger selections completely hands-free for individuals with motor disabilities (e.g., ALS, cerebral palsy).

### ⚡ Phase 3: Quick Phrases & Contextual Smart Assist (`/phrases`)
- **Categorized Speed Phrases:** Instant one-tap phrases for rapid conversation (Emergencies, Assistance, Daily Needs).
- **Custom Phrase Creator:** Allows users to create, tag, and persist personalized frequently-used phrases.
- **Context-Aware Smart Suggestions:** Backend intelligence predicting relevant quick phrases depending on situational context (e.g., *pain*, *greeting*, *help*, *food*).
- **One-Tap Emotion Board:** Instant emotional state broadcast with synthesized voice responses.

### 🤟 Phase 4: Sign AI & Facial Emotion Recognition (`/signs`)
- **On-Device Gesture Recognition:**
  - Uses **MediaPipe Hands** to analyze 21 3D landmarks in real time.
  - Detects letters and common universal sign gestures (*Hello*, *Yes*, *No*, *I Love You*, *OK*, *Peace*, *Help*, *Stop*, *Point*, *Call Me*, etc.) with live confidence percentages.
- **Facial Landmark Emotion Detection:**
  - Uses **MediaPipe FaceMesh** (468 landmarks) to calculate mouth aspect ratios and eyebrow vectors to detect facial expressions (*Happy*, *Sad*, *Surprised*, *Angry*, *Calm*).
- **Live Sign-to-Speech & Captions:** Recognitions are streamed to an integrated captioning overlay and spoken aloud via speech synthesis.
- **Interactive Training & Practice:** Practice mode with sign dictionary and historical progress tracking.

### 📖 Phase 5: Voice-First Screen Reader & Braille Station (`/reader`)
- **Global Accessible Voice Navigation:** Full voice-command listener allowing users to navigate pages, trigger actions, and read screen elements using natural speech.
- **Grade 1 Braille Character Converter:**
  - Converts English text into tactile 6-dot Grade 1 Braille representation in real-time.
  - Ready for hardware integration with physical refreshable Braille displays via USB/Bluetooth.
- **Visual Accessibility Enhancements:**
  - **Focus Spotlight Mode:** Dims background and highlights focused UI elements to assist low-vision users.
  - **Triple-Theme Support:** Dark, Light, and strict high-contrast (black/pure white/cyan/yellow) adhering to **WCAG 2.1 AAA standards**.
  - **Variable Typography:** Instant font scaling (Regular, Large +20%, Extra Large +45%).

### 🌍 Phase 6: Inclusive Community Hub (`/community`)
- **Cross-Disability Social Network:** Allows users with different abilities, caregivers, and allies to connect, share experiences, and exchange assistive tips.
- **Community Feed & Forum:** Post updates, filter by category (*Deaf*, *Mute*, *Blind*, *Tech*, *General*), and like/interact with posts.
- **Disability Profiles & Badges:** Dedicated user profiles highlighting preferred accessibility accommodations and communication modes.
- **Community Activity Dashboard:** Visual metrics tracking sessions completed across all phases.

### 🤖 Specialized Module: Medical Symptom Communicator (`/symptom`)
- **Visual Clinical Triage:** Tailored for non-verbal or distressed patients in emergency or hospital situations.
- **4-Step Intuitive Workflow:**
  1. *Symptom Selector:* One-touch tagging of critical symptoms (Chest Pain, Shortness of breath, Nausea, Dizziness, etc.).
  2. *Anatomical Targeter:* Pinpoints affected body regions (Head, Chest, Abdomen, Limbs).
  3. *Severity & Duration:* Visual 1–10 numeric pain scale with color gradients and symptom duration.
  4. *Clinical Report Generator:* Synthesizes a structured medical brief that can be read aloud, copied for doctors, or downloaded for paramedic handoff.

### 🚨 Specialized Module: One-Touch Emergency SOS (`/emergency`)
- **High-Priority Distress Beacon:** Large, accessible emergency button with audible siren alerts and flashing distress beacon.
- **Automated Geolocation Capture:** Extracts real-time latitude/longitude coordinates from browser GPS.
- **Multi-Channel Dispatch:** Pushes immediate distress alerts to designated emergency contacts and broadcasts over live WebSockets.

### 🌐 Cross-Cutting Platform Capabilities
- **8-Language Multilingual Engine:** Full localized UI and on-the-fly translation covering English and 7 Indian languages (Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Odia, Marathi).
- **Global Screen Reader Context:** Dedicated top-level context (`GlobalScreenReaderContext`) that reads out button labels, route transitions, and UI elements anywhere across the application.
- **Offline & Low-Hardware Optimization:** Operates entirely inside standard modern web browsers without requiring high-end GPUs or dedicated proprietary hardware.
