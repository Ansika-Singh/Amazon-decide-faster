The issue has been resolved.

### What was causing the redirect not to trigger:
1. **CSS Layering & Click Interception**: The `.landing-header` previously had `z-index: 5`, matching the hero content grid (`.product-hero-grid`). Because the hero grid comes later in the HTML and had `162px` of top padding, its invisible padding layer was sitting directly in front of the navigation bar, swallowing mouse clicks before they could reach the links.
2. **Missing `pointer-events: none` on Overlays**: The hero ambient video vignette was also capturing mouse events in that area.

---

### What was fixed:
1. **Elevated Stacking Context**:
   - Set `.landing-header` to `z-index: 60 !important` and `pointer-events: auto !important`.
   - Set `.desktop-landing-nav` to `z-index: 70 !important`.
   - Set `.landing-nav-link` to `z-index: 71 !important` and `cursor: pointer !important`.
   - Added `pointer-events: none !important` to the video vignette and grain overlays so they never block clicks.
2. **Explicit Navigation Handlers**:
   - Attached instant click handlers (`goTo("/upload")`, `goTo("/track")`, `goTo("/dashboard")`) to the links to trigger immediate client-side route transitions when clicked.
3. **Interactive Button Feedback**:
   - Styled the navigation items as pills with translucent glass backgrounds (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), borders, and an active emerald hover glow so you get immediate visual feedback when hovering or clicking.

You can now click **Report Issue**, **Track Status**, and **Trends & Dashboard** at [http://localhost:3000/](http://localhost:3000/) and they will redirect cleanly.
